I am a fourth-year PhD student in the [Linguistics Department](https://ling.bu.edu/graduate/phd-linguistics/) at Boston University, where I am advised by [Neil Myler](https://sites.google.com/site/neilmylerlinguist/). I'm curious about the manner and extent to which languages vary from one another, especially in their morphosyntax. The project on which I currently focus seeks to address this issue as it arises in the morphology of conjugation class membership in verbs of [Greko](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/the-greek-language-of-calabria-jal%C3%B2-tu-vua/PAWBtmtXZ5XahA?hl=en) and Standard Modern Greek (my native language).

In the past, I have also been interested in aspects of [cliticization]({{ '/research/#cliticization' | relative_url }}) in my dialect, [compounding]({{ '/research/#compounding' | relative_url }}) and whether compounds violate the Final-Over-Final Constraint, phonetic correlates of [whistled Greek]({{ '/research/#whistled-greek' | relative_url }}), and [polydefiniteness]({{ '/research/#polydefiniteness' | relative_url }}).

In my free time I maintain a [Facebook (Meta?) page](https://www.facebook.com/glossakoul/) that popularizes general linguistics. I also broadly enjoy board games, broad games, bored games, puns, playing GeoGuessr, and occasionally describing [music in terms of realizational morphology]({{ '/music/' | relative_url }}).
