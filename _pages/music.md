A list of a few well-known music groups in the terminology of realizational morphology. Not meant to be exhaustive or organized, but used to introduce music to a morphologist.

---

**/The Beatles/** &nbsp; `[+OG] ↔ /The Beatles/ / ___ [+good music taste, +for genuinely good people]`<br>
The realization of the [+OG] feature next to the combined span [+good music taste, +for genuinely good people].

**/The Rolling Stones/** &nbsp; `[+OG] ↔ /The Rolling Stones/`<br>
An allomorph of the [+OG] feature that competes with /The Beatles/ for insertion, but (unlike it) doesn't bear the [+for genuinely good people] feature.

**/Green Day/** &nbsp; `[+California, +early teenager punk] ↔ /Green Day/`<br>
Exponent of the features [+California], [+early teenager punk].

**/The Gorillaz/**<br>
A Vocabulary Item that fuses with the linearly immediately preceding morpheme. As such, it can adapt to various music styles.

**/Pulp/**<br>
A morphomic piece whose genius cannot be reduced to any other.

**/Ska-P/**<br>
The product of a post-syntactic rule that reduplicates all Spanish morphemes at an extremely fast-paced rhythm of externalization.

**/Taylor Swift/** &nbsp; `[+sound] ↔ /Taylor Swift/` _(elsewhere)_<br>
The elsewhere-principle exponent of the feature [+sound]. Boring and uninteresting, it wins the competition only in lack of specification for music taste.
